Predict Insurance Premiums with Confidence

This project leverages a trained Random Forest Machine Learning model to predict insurance premium costs based on user inputs like age, BMI, smoking status, and more. It's designed to be fast, reliable, and user-friendly for both customers and insurance professionals.

Try Prediction Now

Why This Project?

Insurance premiums vary due to multiple factors. Our goal is to make premium prediction transparent and accessible. By analyzing real-world data, we help users understand what affects their insurance costs.

How It Works

We trained a Random Forest Regressor on a dataset that includes key insurance features like age, gender, region, smoking habits, BMI, and children. The model then provides accurate premium estimates instantly.
